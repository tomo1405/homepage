About

I was born in 2002 and am from Ehime, Japan. I am currently studying computer science at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST).

As software systems grow increasingly complex, I am interested in the question of how we can assure their quality. My work centers on software engineering, with a particular focus on software testing, code quality assessment, and program analysis and test generation using large language models. I seek reproducible approaches that improve software quality in real-world development.

Through my research, I aim to become an engineer who can explain why a result occurs from a theoretical perspective. My goal is to produce research outcomes that deliver practical value in software development.
